What Im trying to build 

**ShopCircle** is a social commerce platform that blends the engaging experience of social media with the convenience of online shopping. Inspired by Pinterest's clean, photography-first design, the app allows creators, small businesses, and individuals to showcase products through visually appealing posts in a personalized feed. Users can discover trending products, follow their favorite stores, like and save items, interact through comments, and purchase products without leaving the app. The interface emphasizes large product imagery, warm neutral colors, rounded components, and a masonry-style layout to create an immersive browsing experience. With features such as creator profiles, product pages, search and discovery, shopping cart, messaging, and product publishing, ShopCircle provides a seamless ecosystem where social engagement naturally leads to product discovery and shopping. It aims to make online commerce feel as interactive and enjoyable as scrolling through your favorite social media platform.
